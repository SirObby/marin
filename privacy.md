# Privacy Policy

(Last Updated: 05/10/2026)

Thank you for choosing to use Marin. This Privacy Policy describes the information we collect, how we use that information, and what choices you have. This policy applies to all Marin services and any associated services (referred to as the "Service"). If you do not agree with this policy, do not access or use our Services or interact with any other aspect of our business.

## 1. Information We Collect

The Service collects and stores the following information:
- **Discord User IDs** and your chosen language preferences (Locale) to provide localized responses.
- **Saved Favorites** (Anime and Manga IDs from AniList) that you explicitly add to your profile.
- **Discord Guild (Server) IDs** and configuration settings to provide server-specific customizations.

## 2. Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can permanently delete all personal data associated with your Discord User ID at any time by using the `/delete_my_data` command.
Additionally, when the bot is removed from a Discord Server, all configuration data related to that server is automatically purged from our database.
